The actual final bounded grammar recognises 20 additional full-field manufacturer assertions in read-only SQLite screening; all 20 have stored-current flags and historical vegan labels. This repairs interpretation of coordinated dietary attributes such as “Vegan and gluten free, source of protein”. Applicable cited assessments and every safety gate are still required. It creates no standalone promotion shortcut.

The old deterministic shortcut accepted any comma-separated vegan fragment in features/dietary information. A constructed wine with “The packaging is recyclable, vegan.” consequently became vegan/manufacturer. V35 requires a complete list of supported dietary attributes for this shortcut and defers heterogeneous narrative lists to scope assessment.
