## Current policy and implementation

Plain produce without packaging details may qualify by common sense when no material component concern is evidenced, as explicitly clarified by the user. Missing details do not themselves create a blocker or prove packaging absent. Positive reason precedence remains tagged → manufacturer → name → ingredients.
