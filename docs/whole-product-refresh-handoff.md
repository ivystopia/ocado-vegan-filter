## Evidence rules to preserve

Assess the complete sold article, including processing, packaging, pods, filters and other supplied components. Do not invent materials, product-specific assurances or speculative risks. Ordinary produce without an evidenced material component concern can qualify by common sense despite missing packaging details. Missing ingredients alone does not establish a simple product.

Keep all four positive routes with canonical precedence tagged > manufacturer > name > ingredients. Claims must be affirmative, applicable to the complete product and correctly bound to its identity, variant, market and relevant period. Manufacturer confirmation precedes a vegan name. An ingredient list with no recognised animal word is insufficient where relevant sourcing, processing, composition or component questions remain; wine processing and unspecified source-dependent additives are examples. Applicable whole-product assurances can resolve generic uncertainty but cannot erase definite animal evidence.

Correctly matched definite animal ingredients or manufacturing inputs mean nonvegan even with an Ocado vegan tag. Collect confirmed tag contradictions for a report; do not contact Ocado without instructions. Wrong-product identity and unresolved material conflicts mean blocked unknown. Precautionary may-contain warnings are not intentional animal ingredients. Keep the shopper label Unknown vegan and browser precedence recorded nonvegan → blocked unknown → validated positive IDs/applicable official tags → unknown. Runtime names and URL slugs cannot promote.
