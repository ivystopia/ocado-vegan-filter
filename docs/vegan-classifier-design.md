## Evidence policy

The classifier asks whether applicable stored evidence establishes vegan suitability of the complete product, including relevant manufacturing. It never guesses from probability, brand reputation, category alone or absence of an animal word. `unknown` means assessed evidence insufficiency; NULL means work remains. Execution failures remain operational failures and never count as completed unknown decisions.

Scope correction (2026-10-06), policy `whole-product-v2`: assess all supplied components, including packaging, coffee pods and brewing filters. Suitability is for ethical vegans, not only the edible contents. The separate `components` assessment must cover non-edible parts; a food-only claim cannot clear their material uncertainty. A headline fibre/material is not necessarily complete manufactured-component composition. Preserve ordinary-food/common-sense positives without invented risks. Sugar-cane fibre is not refined sugar. Applicable whole-product positive claims remain permitted, subject to correctly matched contrary evidence and scope checks. Earlier fixtures and results remain retained under v1; changed reference expectations require an explicit crosswalk.

Apply this equally to explicitly described bags, cartons, bottles, jars and other packaging formats. Ordinary-food identity establishes the food, not a stated manufactured package's complete composition. Loose ordinary food and food with no evidenced material component concern remain eligible; do not invent a supplied component solely because its absence is unproved. Applicable whole-product claims may resolve generic component uncertainty. Known component resolution facts require interpretation and explicit issue-resolution references rather than a premature rules-only unknown.

The user's subsequent clarification permits plain produce with no packaging details to qualify by common sense when no material component concern is evidenced. Missing packaging information alone is not a blocker. Cite the clear product identity and assess components as established under this permission; do not claim packaging is absent or invent its materials. The annotation prompt states the same threshold explicitly. Earlier automated readings that inferred a blocker solely from missing packaging are retained as superseded interpretations, never silently relabelled.

The no-invented-concern rule also applies to other unambiguous ordinary foods. Protective-atmosphere wording alone does not identify a manufactured component/material or evidence a material concern. It proves neither packaging absence nor suitability. Explicit bags, pods, filters and other described manufactured components still require assessment. Two additional blind source-cited readings retained the plain-coffee reference without changing its frozen evidence; the separately described coffee-bag reference remains unknown.

`tools/vegan_policy.py` defines the shared policy, knowledge registry and structured schema. The permitted positive routes, in priority order, are applicable official Ocado tag (`tagged`), explicit scoped manufacturer confirmation (`manufacturer`), affirmative whole-product vegan name (`name`), and unambiguous ordinary vegan identity/composition with relevant processing/sourcing resolved (`ingredients`). Whole-product claims can resolve generic processing/sourcing uncertainty. They cannot override definite animal content.

Both model stages must inspect and cite all applicable positive bases before selecting a reason. Affirmative producer self-descriptions can establish manufacturer confirmation without the exact words “suitable for vegans”. A producer's Vegan Society approval is manufacturer evidence; only an actual Ocado official flag/tag is tagged evidence. The cited-claim validator recognises a whole-product assertion in a separate sentence from composition wording while retaining restrictive qualifications across the quotation. This is evidence interpretation within a complete validated assessment, not a new conclusive rule or permission to ignore scope, animal content or either independent pass.

Known brand wording cannot supply the product's vegan attribute. For example, “Vegan Foods Shampoo is lightly fragranced” is not manufacturer confirmation; “Vegan Foods Shampoo is vegan” contains an independent assertion. The source-aware validator applies this distinction to full and short cited descriptions and their retained sentence, preserving separate applicable assurances, scope restrictions and definite-animal overrides.

A fast rule's failure to recognise unfamiliar claim wording is not proof that no claim exists. Stored product-information fields and typed product-information rows with possible vegan assertions receive semantic assessment when the rules would otherwise finish unknown. This routing cannot promote a token match and preserves definite-animal decisions, identity blocks and clear component-only or other-product statements. Missing process/source facts without a potentially applicable assertion can still finish as unknown without a model call.

Apply that reason boundary consistently: an applicable declarative producer description identifies its product as a vegan article even if its subject repeats the SKU designation and its predicate discusses flavour, texture or nutrition. That is manufacturer evidence. A quoted title, a name mentioned without an assertion, a serving suggestion or a recipe/component-only statement is insufficient for that route. Independent annotation uses the same distinction, reads the full supplied product information and retains earlier reason disagreements and any corrected references through explicit crosswalks.

Correctly matched definite animal ingredients mean `nonvegan` even if Ocado tags the product vegan. Retain the tag contradiction for a report to Ocado. Suspected mismatched/misjoined product evidence means `unknown` with a promotion block, rather than accusing the wrong product. Other material contradictions, incomplete composition, unresolved source-dependent additives and relevant processing risks mean `unknown`. “May contain milk” is precautionary information, not intentional milk. Standard flour fortification is permitted. A missing ingredients list does not establish simplicity; carrots or a plain leaf salad can qualify from unambiguous identity/composition, while wine, manufactured non-foods and an ambiguous category/name cannot.

Direct punctuation-separated negations such as “Not-Vegan” and “Not: Vegan” cannot become affirmative name claims. “Vegan Formula Shampoo” describes the formula, not the complete article; an independently applicable whole-product tag or manufacturer claim can still qualify. “Not certified vegan” is absence of certification, not proof of animal content. A vegan title cannot erase a contradictory unqualified animal-food full identity. Source declarations such as “derived from milk” and actual animal inputs explicitly used during manufacture override positive claims. A negative suitability claim conflicting with a positive claim remains unknown unless applicable definite animal evidence settles it. The final gate rejects unsupported negative assertions as well as unsupported positives, without promoting agreed unknowns.

Name matching identifies candidates, not accepted positives. Name-only candidates always enter the independent model path to assess assertion polarity and whole-article scope, including higher-precedence manufacturer evidence. A normal affirmative vegan SKU name asserts suitability for the named product; a food noun or ordinary pack-format suffix alone does not restrict it to edible contents. Actual restrictive evidence is required. Generic missing compound/component facts can be covered by a whole-product claim; definite contrary evidence cannot. Confidence measures evidence sufficiency under the policy: supported vegan decisions are certain, while material uncertainty means unknown. Both positive passes must cite their proposed positive basis; the final gate requires cited name support and rejects rule-only name shortcuts. Negated and hedged names such as the hypothetical “Never Vegan Wine” or “Probably Vegan Wine” do not establish suitability. These are test inputs, not proposed output labels. The user retained `Unknown vegan` for uncertainty and declined a separate probable label for now.

Declaration parsing keeps complete source text for review. Word boundaries prevent “made without” becoming “made with”; separate precautionary-allergen clauses cannot extend another intentional target. Explicit no-meat/alternative and non-dairy ice-cream headings resolve only the referenced token, retaining every adjacent and nested animal ingredient. This includes alternative/substitute to/for egg(s) in composition and product descriptions; it cannot erase separately declared eggs, milk, gelatine or nested animal constituents, and cannot establish suitability without a permitted whole-product basis.

Check product form as well as name: a volume-labelled bottled product whose ingredient list describes gelatine capsules/capsule shells has an unresolved identity/formulation mismatch. Block promotion as unknown and retain it in the separate tagged-identity review report. A correctly matched capsule product containing gelatine remains nonvegan and belongs in the definite animal/tag contradiction report. Likewise, an opaque volume-labelled title with a conflicting solid-meat full identity requires identity review; an agreeing meat title or actual soup recipe still supplies animal evidence.

The small source-backed risk registry identifies mechanisms and acceptable resolutions, not categorical non-vegan verdicts. Registry v7 includes clarification risks for cider, beer, vinegar and juice, and maple-syrup defoaming. Final filtering, plant origin or distillation alone does not establish animal-free precursor processing. Applicable whole-product claims can resolve generic uncertainty; ingredient/process facts must cover the relevant production chain. Sources: https://vegan.org/vegan-faq/clarifying-agents and https://www.ever.it/en/products/eversol/ . Registry v7 also covers planting-seed treatments/coatings/binders, supported by the primary Incotec patent https://patents.google.com/patent/US20180303023A1/en . Plant/native/organic origin alone does not resolve these inputs. Untreated and uncoated facts, suitable complete treatment facts or an applicable whole-product claim can resolve the mechanism. Edible seeds, harvested ordinary produce and tools are excluded. This does not establish that a particular SKU has animal inputs. Its absence is not proof of vegan suitability. Models assess case-specific risks beyond the registry. Do not add speculative risks that invalidate all ordinary foods, or generalise one manufacturer's reassurance to another product.

Check a purported ordinary food against its full stored identity and contrary detail/category evidence. A mango flavour name on a hard seltzer or pineapple in a gin-liqueur full name cannot establish plain fruit. Likewise, mixed “Contains Milk” and factory-handling text is qualified/conflicting evidence, not an obvious intentional ingredient by itself. A separately declared milk ingredient remains definite animal content. Retain mixed fields intact for assessment rather than deleting whichever phrase disagrees with a preferred result.

A source assurance must resolve the actual uncertain ingredient: vegetable oil does not establish the source of E471, and resolving E471 does not resolve E422. “100% plant-based formula” is composition evidence, not automatically a whole-product manufacturing assurance. A vegan-suitability FAQ can qualify through its correctly scoped question and answer together. For manufactured non-food articles, a material entry alone does not prove the entire article's composition; explicit complete-composition evidence or an applicable whole-product claim can qualify.

Known animal E numbers include E120, E901, E904 and E1105. The E1105 food-additive specification identifies hens' egg whites as the source; do not generalise this to every biological use of the word lysozyme. Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/231/pdfs/eur_20120231_adopted_en.pdf (E1105 definition, printed page L83/276); current GB authorisation index: https://data.food.gov.uk/regulated-products/food_authorisations/e-1105. The registry is deliberately bounded, and models must assess other unresolved additives instead of interpreting absence from the registry as proof.

## Pipeline and model contract

Deterministic rules first resolve clear positives, negatives and missing-fact unknowns. Interpretively difficult scope, identity and composition enter the model queue. The universal final policy gate runs after model merging and before every canonical write, including manufacturer imports.

Two independent primary calls assess identical frozen evidence. Only a raw status/reason disagreement triggers exactly two fresh arbitration calls. Arbitration sees evidence, not the primary conclusions, uses the same whole-product policy and can accept all four positive routes. Agreed primary unknowns do not escalate. Arbitration disagreement remains unknown, without recursion. A policy gate may reject an agreed unsupported positive without creating another escalation stage. Substantive issues and unresolved dimensions raised by either agreeing pass survive merging.

## Persistence and evidence lifecycle

Products store canonical status/reason, classifier/policy version, per-product evidence hash, promotion-block flag and canonical audit ID. Runs and audit rows also record knowledge/schema/prompt versions. `vegan_model_calls` stores calls once; `vegan_audit_model_calls` links affected product audit rows, avoiding duplicated batch accounting. Failed calls receive audit records without replacing the canonical decision or clearing pending work.

`product_vegan_conflicts` preserves open/resolved conflicts and tag/animal contradictions. Manufacturer website captures retain URL, retrieval time, full text/hash, publisher/market identity and exact product binding. Assertions support positive and negative status, active/revoked/superseded state and named supersession. Withdrawal invalidates dependent canonical evidence while preserving source/audit history. The storage helpers do not replace scope review. New retrieval dates alone do not override specific contrary evidence. Legacy binding compatibility is limited to the earlier context projection; real identity/recipe/pack/publisher changes still invalidate evidence.

The classifier never researches live websites. Manufacturer research and benchmark research are explicit separate workflows; new external facts enter a frozen classifier context only in an explicitly evidence-augmented case.

Supply every retained manufacturer capture associated with the product, including pages that were not selected for a previous positive quotation. Do not expose old research conclusions as evidence. Preserve capture hashes and catalogue-match/change state; changed associations cannot independently justify promotion. Repeated complete pages are transported once per model batch, with logical per-product references retained. Quote validation permits exact visible source words after removal of retrieval-line/citation/Markdown decoration, without fuzzy matching or alteration of the retained raw capture. Models must read complete pages and quote concise excerpts.

Schema 5 assesses identity, composition, sourcing, processing and supplied components. It requires a separate `source_applicability` assessment for each website source relied on: product, variant, market and period, with source references for every dimension and product/source identity references for the product match. Both agreeing passes must establish applicability; an isolated vegan quotation, a generic matching title or a catalogue association hash is insufficient. Explicit colour-selector and vintage mismatches are independently rejected. Inclusive range evidence may cover colours but cannot erase a stated vintage limitation. Manufacturer imports use the same assessment and gate, and rebind pointers after persistence by source URL and complete capture hash. Catalogue identity bindings exclude external captures to avoid circular invalidation when evidence is imported. Product contexts include package type and current vintage. These checks constrain semantic mistakes; they do not prove arbitrary model interpretations or undisclosed manufacturing facts.
